## KILLED

**LLM Council / anti-sycophancy review harness as a sellable template** (killed 2026-07-31) — The `/council` build is genuinely useful internally, but the market check says the sellable version is already free everywhere and was commoditized within weeks of Karpathy publishing it. (1) The source repo is public and free: [karpathy/llm-council](https://github.com/karpathy/llm-council). (2) A Claude-native port — the exact shape Gray built — is already being given away as a free skill and free write-ups: [mikareyes.com](https://mikareyes.com/ai/how-to-run-llm-council-in-claude), [abhijayvuyyuru.substack.com](https://abhijayvuyyuru.substack.com/p/i-let-5-ais-fight-over-my-decisions), [learnaiwithmariah.com](https://learnaiwithmariah.com/guides/llm-council-prompt/), [solopreneurcode.substack.com](https://solopreneurcode.substack.com/p/how-i-built-the-llm-council-inside). The one competitor Gray saw giving it away as a lead magnet is not an outlier — it is the entire market. (3) Claude Code agent-definition packs specifically are a free-by-default category: [VoltAgent/awesome-claude-code-subagents](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-claude-code-subagents) ships 100+ subagents free. (4) The paid layer of the "AI that disagrees with you" category is occupied by funded SaaS advisory-board products (OpenSERV, Alystron, AppDuck — see [buildmvpfast.com](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/ai-cofounder-startup-coaching-tools-replace-advisory-boards-2026)), which is a SaaS-with-support shape the side-doable gate rules out anyway. **Not killed:** using `/council` as *content* — a build-in-public video on how it works and what it killed is a legitimate use of the same asset, and costs Gray nothing extra.
