I had lunch with my mentor and his co-founder. They run an agency that employs over 800 people.

I was expecting them to talk about business and money and growth.

The entire time, they were just incredibly curious about me and my business.

These are people who could have retired years ago and been on vacation for the rest of their lives.

Here I was, sitting there with nothing to provide them. Yet they cared so deeply about me.

Through that one conversation it became so clear they were in the business because of the people.

If you make your North Star people instead of money, you'll really never want to retire.

Seeing that firsthand made me realize what my true North Star was.
