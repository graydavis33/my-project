I was close to making over a million dollars liquid at 17.

I'm so glad I didn't.

Since the age of 10, my entire identity was rooted in wanting to prove myself.

Elementary school, I just wanted to fit in.
Middle school, my friends started caring about grades, so I wanted to prove I was as smart as them.
High school, everything was about the future, so I needed to prove I was thinking about mine.

That's when I started an NFT project. To prove to the world I was capable of making money.

I put everything into it. We had some of the biggest names in the world backing us. We'd spent so much money developing the project.

Right as we were about to launch, the NFT market collapsed.

Overnight, I lost the identity I'd spent 12 years building.

People talk about divine interventions. Looking back, it was probably the best thing that ever happened to me.

Everything I'd built on ego and proving myself had collapsed.

Making money to prove yourself usually ends up doing nothing.

Doing it for yourself is what actually makes you happy at the end of the day.
