Three purchases that changed how I work and sleep.

1. Monitor light
Clips right onto my screen. One button and my whole face lights up. No more bad lighting on calls.

2. Whiteboard
I write the day down, look at it, cross it off. Nothing on a screen ever feels the same to me.

3. Eye mask
Cost a ridiculous amount, worth every cent. New York never actually gets dark, so I slap it on and sleep like a baby.

None of these are complicated. They just work for me.
