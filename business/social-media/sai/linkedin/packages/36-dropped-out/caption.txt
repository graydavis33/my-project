I dropped out of college, did high school online, and gave up my social life to build a business.

If I could go back, I wouldn't do it the same way.

Not because it didn't work.

Because I thought it would take months. It took years.

When you first start, everything feels close, so you convince yourself you can shortcut it.

What changed for me was the timeline in my head.

I stopped counting in weeks and months.
Now I think in 3, 5, 10 years.

The bad days stopped feeling like the end.
The slow months stopped feeling like failure.

I'd do it all again.
I'd just tell myself one thing first: it's going to take way longer than you think.
