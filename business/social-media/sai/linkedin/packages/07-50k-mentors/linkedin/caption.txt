I've spent over $50,000 on courses and gurus.

Three entrepreneurs taught me more for free.

1. Sharran Srivatsaa - operationalize kindness.

He was there for us in the early days when he had no reason to be.
Still checks in now with zero incentive.

Goodwill is worth 100x more than followers, fame, or money.
Because it's the intrinsic reward for actually changing someone's life.

2. Justin Kan, founder of Twitch - bet on yourself.

He was kind enough to write my college rec letter.
Having someone like him bet on me made me realize I need to take more bets on myself.

3. Mark Cuban - give back.

I cold emailed him when I was 18.
To this day, he still responds. No incentive, no reason.

It made me realize that if I ever reach a certain level, I don't need a reason to be nice to people.
Just give back without expectation.

So to save you $50,000:

Operationalize kindness.
Bet on yourself.
Give back when you have no reason to.
