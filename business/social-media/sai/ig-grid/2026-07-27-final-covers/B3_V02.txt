What are making 2000 ads teach you that you can't learn making it to?
The hardest part about building a business that you want to eventually scale is ensuring
that every single unit is as good as the last. A lot of times what ends up happening is
you scale versus use. But the product or service that you deliver just gets worse and worse.
And so the difference is building systems that make sure that every single unit delivers.
And that is just as good as if you were to make 10,000. When you fundamentally aim
that one unit and you figure out how to scale that one unit, that's when you can build
really big and ensure that nothing breaks.
