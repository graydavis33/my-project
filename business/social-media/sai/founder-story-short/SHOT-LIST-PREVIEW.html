Open decisions / review flags

  1. Script still lives only in the 7/28 Mac chat. The Notion page says "paste it here when ready" — until it lands, VO beats can't be locked to shots. Highest priority.
  2. Board continuity — pick ONE hero surface. Refs mix whiteboard + magnets (2, 3), corkboard + pins (4, 15), and white wall + paper (9, 12, 16). Yarn needs pins to anchor, so magnets can't carry shots 8/9/12 — recommend one big corkboard/foam board for everything.
  3. When does the red yarn first appear? Shot 3's close variant already shows yarn with only one photo up. Suggest: board stays clean through shot 4; yarn enters at shot 8/9 as the "connecting the dots" act.
  4. Board states must be shot in order (or pre-planned): empty+1 (3, 2) → filling (4) → milestones+yarn (8) → full web (9) → complete (11, 12, 14, 15, 16). One dressed board, staged progressively.
  5. Print-prop list needed: young-Sai photo, laptop-grind, milestone-celebration, moving-boxes, team headshots, group shots — real photos from Sai's archive. Sai has to supply the childhood/teenage ones; flag lead time. Plus red yarn + push pins.
  6. Shot 1 casting: young-Sai stand-in — real kid actor, or AI plate composited? Biggest open production question.
  7. Shot 5 variant decision: floating-memories composite vs social-feed wall projection — pick one.
  8. Shot 6 could be free: matches the existing red-light bed-journaling scene (C2415–C2419, filmed Apr 29). Pull from library instead of reshooting?
  9. 5 + 7 are both floating-memory VFX (two angles). Fine, but it's 2× compositing cost — decide if both survive the edit.
  10. No ending bookend. Shot 16 (photo swap) closes the board story, but nothing returns to the shot-1 stage. Optional shot 17: back on the dark stage, young Sai smiles / nods at current Sai — closes the loop.
  11. VFX list (post): flicker morph (1) · floating memories (5/7) · echo-trail + memory-flash overlays (9) · photo-comes-alive (14). Three need clean plates on set.
  12. Nothing specified for music / VO record / sound design anywhere yet.
