WHAT I NEED FROM YOU BY SATURDAY, AUGUST 8

This is the one thing that can push the shoot, so it comes first.

1. Archival photos. Old photos of you from your phone, family albums, or old
   drives. Younger years especially. These get printed and pinned to the board,
   so real photos matter a lot more than anything I could fake.

2. A real team photo. Right now the only thing that exists is Google Meet
   screenshots and those will not work. Three separate shots resolve on this
   photo, including the final image of the whole video, so it needs to be a real
   one.

Why Saturday: once you send them, I build a contact sheet for you to approve,
then prep them to exact print size, then get them printed matte at 4x6, then
press them flat overnight. That is two round trips between us plus a print run.
Saturday is the last day that chain still fits before Tuesday.
